naʻau kombucha

飲むだけじゃない。自分の身体に気づく、kombucha。

Three directions for a small kombucha maker in Nagoya: one that evolves the brand naʻau already has, and two new ones. Each is shown on packaging, print, social and the website, grounded in research on how small craft brands earn trust.

01

What we heard

The name

naʻau is Hawaiian for the gut, the heart and the inner voice. kimy chose it in a second, after 直感kombucha, instinct kombucha and gut feeling kombucha all felt wrong.

The maker

kimy is a nurse and gut-health advisor who brews kombucha in Nagoya and teaches it in workshops. Her voice is warm, direct and a little playful.

The feeling

Grounded, trustworthy and hand-made; quiet rather than corporate. Kombucha is not everything, but it can be where you begin to listen to your body.

02

What the research says

Everything in the three directions follows these findings. Items still to confirm with naʻau are marked in the notes.

Packaging

  • 330 ml amber glass: it blocks UV and protects the live culture.
  • Matte, water-resistant label stock: condensation ruins uncoated paper.
  • Back label carries the 一括表示 at 8 pt minimum, plus 要冷蔵, ‘don’t shake’ and a hand-stamped batch box.
  • Kombucha under 1% alcohol is 清涼飲料水; test each batch, as it can ferment past 1% in the bottle.

Sources: Mountain Culture · Health-Ade · MHLW

Print

  • A5 is the standard hand-out; shop cards are 91 × 55 mm; 3 mm bleed.
  • Workshop details in the expected order: 日時, 場所, 対象, 定員, 内容, 参加費（税込）, 申込方法.
  • QR codes for LINE and Instagram on the back.

Sources: A5 sizes

Social

  • Instagram's grid shows 3:4 since 2025: design at 1080 × 1440, key content inside a 4:5 safe area.
  • Stories and Reels at 9:16.
  • Batch posts, process, the maker's hands, workshop carousels with details on the last slide; LINE for restock alerts.

Sources: Post sizes

Website and shop

  • Prices must be shown tax-inclusive (総額表示, since 2021).
  • Put クール便 cost, dispatch days and a delivery date next to the cart button: 64% of shoppers look for shipping cost on the product page.
  • Footer: 特定商取引法に基づく表記, 配送・返品, プライバシー. BASE supports クール便 and PayPay.

Sources: Baymard · 総額表示

Colour and trust

  • Undyed, low-saturation colours read as more natural and raise willingness to pay for healthy food.
  • Matte packs read as more natural than glossy ones.
  • Saturated blue reads ‘competent’ and corporate; warm off-white and warm ink read hand-made.

Sources: Marozzo et al. 2020 · Matte matters

Words we can and can't use

  • No health-effect claims on a normal food: avoid 便秘解消, 腸内環境改善, 免疫力UP.
  • kimy's nursing background belongs in her bio, never beside product benefits, and never as ‘監修’.
  • Check with 名古屋市保健所 before any label says 非加熱 (unheated).

Sources: Saitama guidance

05

Decisions for naʻau

  1. Choose a direction, and a palette for it.
  2. If Lenora or Ogg: license the font for logo use (Lenora needs the Logo and Merchandise add-ons; Ogg's desktop licence covers logos) and confirm trademark registration in Japan in writing with the foundry.
  3. Confirm with 名古屋市保健所 how the kombucha may be described (heated or not) and the 一括表示 wording.
  4. Send scans of kimy's handwriting and plan a photography day: hands, tea, the pour, the brewing table.
  5. Choose the shop platform (BASE handles クール便 and PayPay) and set up LINE.
