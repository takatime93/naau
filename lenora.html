Concept A · Lenora

NO. 027 · BOTTLED FOR ONE

Bottled for one

A made-to-order atelier in mixed media: the mark printed on real creased paper, details typed on slips and taped on, names written in pencil, a vermilion 一本 seal pressed across the pieces. The drop from the wordmark becomes the count of fermenting days, one per day, filled when the batch is bottled; every label is made out to one person and stamped with a vermilion 一本 seal, the only colour besides the amber glass.

Notes

The wordmark is shown from your Lenora setting with a redrawn water-drop ʻokina. License Lenora with Superior Type’s Logo and Merchandise add-ons, then reset it as vector.

Borrowed, not copied: from references we took fine line work and stamped labels as principles. Our version counts fermenting days with the drop from our own wordmark, and seals each label with a 一本 hanko.

The camellia (tea flower) is drawn for this concept; a final version would be drawn by hand, then the 一本 seal carved as a real hanko so every label is stamped at the counter.
